# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This collection describes the repeatable project management processes used by OctoAcme to move work from an initial idea through planning, delivery, release, and continuous improvement. The approach emphasizes customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety.

## Project Management Processes Summary

OctoAcme begins with **initiation**, where teams validate the business need, identify stakeholders, define measurable success criteria, outline a timeline, and decide whether the work should proceed to planning. During **planning**, the team turns the approved initiative into an actionable backlog, defines acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done, estimates scope, identifies dependencies and risks, and maps milestones and releases.

During **execution and tracking**, teams deliver small, testable increments using a project board, regular standups, pull requests, automated checks, and demos or reviews. Project managers and product leaders maintain alignment through status updates, delivery syncs, risk reviews, and clear escalation paths. Risks are recorded with owners and mitigation plans, while blockers can be escalated from the delivery team to the Project Manager, Product Lead, and sponsor as appropriate.

Before **release and deployment**, the team confirms that acceptance criteria are complete, CI and security checks pass, smoke tests are prepared, release notes are drafted, and rollback plans are documented. After each sprint, release, milestone, or incident, the team holds a retrospective to capture what went well, identify improvements, assign action items, and measure their impact. Core roles include Project Managers, Product Managers, Developers, QA and testing contributors, and stakeholders, each with defined responsibilities for delivery, quality, communication, and decision-making.
